# Sales

## Sales Funnel
1.  **Traffic:** Main source is Facebook Marketplace. Secondary sources are Facebook Page and Groups. Future: Ads.
2.  **Discovery:** Customer sees Marketplace listing, shared post, or page post.
3.  **Conversation:** Customer messages. **This is where most sales happen** (not through checkout or catalog).
4.  **Conversion:** Customer picks up OR uses delivery.

## Inbox System Flow
*   **First Reply:** “Disponible para entrega inmediata…”
*   **Delivery Reply:** Pickup + delivery explanation.
*   **Purchase Reply:** Anticipo (deposit) explanation.
*   **Future Automations:** Delivery FAQ, Catalog browsing, Payment options, Human handoff.
