# Products

## Inventory Structure

### A) Repeatable Stock Products
Products that can be reordered repeatedly.
*   **Examples:** Organizers, Kitchen storage, Bathroom storage, Home gadgets, Cleaning products, Phone accessories, Pet accessories, Useful impulse products.
*   **Goals:** Predictable inventory, repeat sales, ads, catalog.
*   **Location:** Facebook Catalog (Collections: *Hogar y Organización, Gadgets y Tecnología, Mascotas, Bienestar y Utilidades, Ofertas*).
*   **Note:** The Catalog is a Sales Support Tool, not a traffic source.

### B) One-Off Products
*   **Examples:** Furniture, Used electronics, Decor, Random deals, Marketplace finds, Liquidation items.
*   **Goals:** Fast cash flow, attention, traffic, local demand.
*   **Location:** Facebook Marketplace.
*   **Note:** Mark these as sold when gone. Do not clutter the main catalog with one-off items.
