# Vision

## One-Sentence Business Definition
Bazarito Cancún is a local discovery-commerce business that combines Marketplace treasure hunting with a curated catalog of useful repeatable products, generating sales primarily through conversations, fast local delivery, and trust-based buying.

## Positioning
**"Useful finds for everyday life in Cancún"**

People should think: *"I wonder what Bazarito has today."*

**What we are NOT:**
- "Online Store"
- "Furniture Store"
- "Marketplace Reseller"

## Current State Analysis
### Strengths
- Local focus
- Fast delivery
- Diverse inventory
- Good margins
- Marketplace experience
- Able to source products

### Bottlenecks
1. **Inventory Tracking:** No centralized system yet.
2. **Product Sourcing:** Occasionally selling out of winning products.
3. **Content Engine:** Not yet systematic.
4. **Reviews:** Need more buyer reviews and social proof.
