# Bazarito Cancún — Business & Brand Overview

> [!NOTE]
> This document serves as the "Master Playbook" for Bazarito Cancún. It combines the core business model, sales strategy, and the newly established brand identity guidelines into a single source of truth.

## 🎯 1. Business Definition & Positioning
**"Useful finds for everyday life in Cancún"** (*"Finds útiles para tu día a día"*)

Bazarito Cancún is a local discovery-commerce business. It combines the excitement of "treasure hunting" on Facebook Marketplace with a curated, web-based catalog of highly useful, repeatable products. 

**What we are NOT:**
- A generic online store with automated checkout
- A dropshipper (we hold local inventory)
- A traditional furniture store

**The Customer Thought:** *"I wonder what Bazarito has today."*

---

## 🎨 2. Brand Identity & Design (New!)
Our visual identity is designed to feel vibrant, local, and friendly, moving away from generic corporate e-commerce.

### The "Sticker" Aesthetic
- **Visuals:** Uses floating "peel-off" stickers (emojis on white cards with soft shadows, like 🌮, 📱, 📦).
- **Shapes:** Everything interactive (buttons, badges) uses fully rounded pill shapes. No sharp corners.
- **Shadows:** We use colorful, tinted glows instead of harsh gray drop shadows (e.g., an orange button has a soft orange glow).

### Color Palette
- **Brand Yellow (`#FFD000`):** The primary brand color. Used for accents, the mobile browser theme bar, and main CTA buttons (like the Product Card Price button).
- **Brand Teal (`#1A7A6D`):** Used for trust signals, primary typography highlights, and "Stock" badges.
- **Brand Orange (`#E84B09`):** High-urgency color used for "Sold" overlays, "SALE" badges, and unique items.
- **Background (`#FFFBEE`):** A warm cream base, much friendlier than stark white.

### Typography & Tone
- **Fonts:** `Outfit` (Headlines, badges, prices) and `Inter` (Body text, descriptions).
- **Voice:** Conversational, helpful, and honest. Treat the customer like a neighbor.

## 📦 3. Inventory Strategy

### A) Repeatable Stock Products
Products that can be reordered and kept in stock.
*   **Categories:** Hogar, Gadgets, Mascotas, Bienestar, Moda y Personal (👗 New!).
*   **Goal:** Predictable inventory, repeat sales.
*   **Web Display:** Shows a teal `Stock` badge.

### B) One-Off Products
Unique items, liquidations, or random deals.
*   **Goal:** Fast cash flow, generating traffic/attention on Marketplace.
*   **Web Display:** Shows an orange `Única` badge. When sold, they are automatically hidden from the public catalog to keep the store looking fresh.

---

## 🚦 4. Sales Funnel & Tech Stack

Our newly built Web App (`smileygz.github.io/Bazarito-cancun`) acts as the anchor for the business, but **WhatsApp/Messenger remains the checkout.**

1.  **Traffic:** Facebook Marketplace (One-offs) & Facebook/Instagram pages.
2.  **Discovery:** Customer browses the Web Catalog.
3.  **Conversion (Web):** Customer clicks the Yellow Price Button, opens the product details, and clicks **"Preguntar"**, which redirects them to Messenger/WhatsApp pre-filled with the product name.
4.  **Closing (Admin):** Admin confirms stock and logs the sale via the Web App's `Registrar Venta` modal.
5.  **Confirmation:** Admin clicks the WhatsApp button to auto-send the delivery/pickup confirmation "machote" to the client.

## 🚚 5. Delivery System & Fees
- **Pickup:** 📍 Región 96 (Free, ready in 15–30 min).
- **Delivery Fees (Added to total revenue in App):**
  - 1–6 km → $50
  - 6–10 km → $80
- **Night Fee:** Flat $100 after 8 PM.
- **Payment Methods:** Mercado Pago, Cash on Delivery (requires $50 deposit), Cash on Pickup.

## 🚀 6. Roadmap: Next Steps
